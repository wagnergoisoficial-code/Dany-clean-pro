1. Introduction

Dany Clean Pro values your trust. This Privacy Policy details the frameworks and digital protocols governing the acquisition, storage, preservation, and utilization of contact parameters and structural data provided through our platforms or team communications.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following classes of data to securely coordinate premium cleaning jobs:

  • Contact Identifiers: Your full name, telephone number, mailing/physical address, and email addresses.
  • Booking Details: Selected dates, preferred time constraints, number of rooms, commercial/residential parameters, and special request comments.
  • Billing Assets: Information strictly processed securely through encrypted third-party merchant channels. No payment credit numbers are retained on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Data

The information we gather is exclusively leveraged to:

Service Scheduling

Securing booking slots, assigning coordinators, and planning regional transit routes.

Automated Alerts

Sending live dispatch updates, ETA alerts, booking confirmations, and follow-up surveys.

4. SMS and TCPA Compliance

Dany Clean Pro is fully committed to compliance under the United States Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and applicable Connecticut communication regulations. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Consent, Help, and Opt-Out Protocols:

  • Consent Authorization: By providing your mobile telephone number on any form or lead collector on our website (https://danycleanpro.com), you provide written consent to receive operational text messages and MMS related to estimates, schedules, team ETAs, confirmations, and follow-ups from Dany Clean Pro.
  • Opt-Out Protocol: You can cancel our text messaging connection at any time. Simply reply STOP to any SMS you receive. After sending "STOP", we will transmit a concluding text confirming you have been cleanly opted out.
  • Carrier Rates: Message and data rates as applied by your wireless provider may apply under standard mobile parameters.
  • Frequency Guarantee: Text messages are dispatched only as logically needed to organize scheduled operations. We do not dispatch unsolicited mass promotional text streams.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

To optimize client-access channels and coordinate regional services, we use cookies and secure localStorage caches. This allows our portal to remember active preferences and speed up booking inquiries.

6. Third-Party Sharing & Infrastructure

To execute regular operation layers, some data is processed through safe cloud partners (e.g. Google Firebase, Twilio). SMS opt-in data, consent, and mobile information are kept strictly confidential and will NOT be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional reasons!

7. Limitation of Liability

We deploy industry-standard SSL safety barriers. However, no internet-facing terminal is 100% impenetrable. Under Connecticut rules, Dany Clean Pro shall not be liable for any third-party service interruptions or cloud outages.

8. Independent Contractors

Vetted independent professionals connected through our platform strictly follow our high quality baselines, safety checks, and checklists. Physical damage liabilities or individual on-site safety procedures remain the responsibility of the assigned cleaner.

9. Platform Security & Prohibited Activities

10. Data Retention Policy

Client records are stored in direct compliance with legal US financial auditing guidelines and bookkeeper tax codes. If you request full data deletion, archival overrides are executed.

11. Your Legal Rights & Data Controls

12. Children's Privacy
